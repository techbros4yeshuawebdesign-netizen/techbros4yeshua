HOW ONE GETS BUILT

STEP 01

The tortilla

Corn masa pressed on the truck and hit on the flat top until it puffs. Never from a bag.

STEP 02

Off the mesquite

Meat comes off the grill, gets chopped on the block and goes straight in. No holding trays.

STEP 03

Onion & cilantro

Diced fine, both raw, both this morning. This is where a taco stops tasting like a restaurant.

STEP 04

Salsa, your call

Verde is bright, roja will get your attention, habanero we only give you if you ask twice.

STEP 05

Lime, and go

Squeeze it before you walk away. Fifteen seconds from the window is the best it will ever be.

THE TRUCK RULES

Five things we don't bend on

01

Masa, pressed here

Tortillas get made on the truck all day. If we run out of masa, we close early. It has happened.

02

Mesquite, not gas

Real wood on the grill. It is slower, it is more work, and you can taste the difference from the sidewalk.

03

Salsa made at 6 AM

Four salsas, roasted and blended every morning. Whatever is left at close gets thrown out.

04

Order goes on the grill

Nothing sits in a warmer. You wait a few minutes because it is being cooked for you, right then.

05

We sell out, we sell out

We would rather hand you a sign than a taco we are not proud of. Come back tomorrow, we will be there.

FROM THE LINE

What people say

SINCE DAY ONE

One truck, one family

Taco Michael started with a flat top, a propane tank and a recipe handed down through the family. We cook the way we cook at home: nothing frozen, salsas made fresh every morning, tortillas pressed on the truck.

You'll usually find Michael at the window taking the order and calling it back to the grill. If the line is long, it's worth it. If we sell out early, come back tomorrow — we will.

“If it isn't good enough for my own table, it doesn't go out the window.”

— Michael, owner
