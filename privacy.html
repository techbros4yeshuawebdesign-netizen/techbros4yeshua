Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

The short version. We collect what we need to build and run your website and to talk with you. We don't sell your information or use it for ads. Ask us any time and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (passwords are stored encrypted by our database provider; we cannot see them).
  • Business details you give us: business name, phone number, what you want on your website, and messages you send us.
  • Files you upload: photos, logos and documents for your website. Photos you send for your website may be published on it.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We see whether a payment went through, but never your full card number.
  • Messages from your website's visitors: when someone uses the contact form on your website, we store what they enter (such as name, email, phone and message) so we can deliver it to you.
  • Basic activity: things like when you signed in or opened your preview, so we can support you.

How we use it

  • To make your plan, preview and website, and to apply your changes.
  • To send you emails about your account, such as your preview being ready, edits being done, or a new message from your website.
  • To handle billing and keep the service secure.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Stripe: processes payments.
  • Google: Gmail for sending emails, and Google Fonts for the typefaces on our pages.
  • Anthropic (Claude): an AI tool we use to help write plans and build websites from the details you give us.
  • GitHub: hosts our website.
  • Tawk.to: the live chat on our home page, if you use it.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your account is active, and afterwards for as long as needed for records the law requires us to keep (such as billing). Unaccepted previews may be deleted after 90 days.

Your choices

  • You can see and update most of your information in your client portal.
  • Email us to get a copy of your information or to have your account and files deleted.
  • California residents have rights to know, delete and correct personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using them. We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and email active clients.
